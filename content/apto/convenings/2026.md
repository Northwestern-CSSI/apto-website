# Building a shared evidence agenda

## Summary

The 2026 APTO annual convening brings the cohort together to compare methods, connect project evidence, and identify opportunities for shared research infrastructure.

## Discussion

The program will focus on common approaches to emerging-technology detection, technology-outcome measurement, scalable evidence pipelines, and forecasting. Teams will also compare validation strategies and identify practical ways to make APTO data, models, and tools useful across institutions.
