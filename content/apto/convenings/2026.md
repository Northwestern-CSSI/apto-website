# Building a shared evidence agenda

## Summary

The 2026 APTO annual convening brought the cohort together to share progress, compare methods, connect project evidence, and identify opportunities for common research infrastructure.

## Discussion

Teams discussed emerging-technology detection, technology-outcome measurement, scalable evidence pipelines, causal modeling, and forecasting. Sessions also examined validation strategies and practical ways to make APTO data, models, and tools useful across institutions.
