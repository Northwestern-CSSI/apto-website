# Multiobjective Technology Outcomes

## Description

Technological progress rarely unfolds along a single dimension, and improvement in one area may reinforce or constrain progress in another. This capability studies change across perovskite solar cells, superconducting qubits and materials, artificial intelligence, processors, manufacturing, and other domains. Cross-domain evidence shows that progress is often punctuated: long periods of limited improvement are interrupted by clusters of breakthroughs shaped by radical innovations and incremental refinements.

## Use case

Compare technology trajectories across capability, production, and use; identify tradeoffs and complementarities; and develop a richer picture of how multiple outcome dimensions evolve together.
