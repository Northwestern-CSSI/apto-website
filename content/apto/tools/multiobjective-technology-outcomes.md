# Multi-Objective Technology Outcomes Tool

## Description

Technological progress rarely unfolds along a single dimension, and improvement in one area may reinforce or constrain progress in another. To understand these interactions, our team is studying technological change across perovskite solar cells, superconducting qubits and materials, artificial intelligence, processors, manufacturing, and other domains.

One cross-domain analysis of millions of solutions found that progress is typically punctuated: long periods of limited improvement are interrupted by clusters of breakthroughs shaped by radical innovations and incremental refinements. These patterns create some near-term predictability while making longer-term trajectories more uncertain.

Together, these findings provide a foundation for moving beyond individual performance frontiers. The tool will integrate measures of technology capability, production, and use, allowing users to compare trajectories, identify tradeoffs and complementarities, and develop a richer understanding of how technologies evolve.

## Use case

Compare technology trajectories across capability, production, and use; identify tradeoffs and complementarities; and develop a richer picture of how multiple outcome dimensions evolve together.
