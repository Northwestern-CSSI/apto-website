# How technology becomes capability, production, and use

## Introduction

The Assessing and Predicting Technology Outcomes program supports evidence, models, data sets, and tools for understanding how investments in science and technology shape technical capability, production, and use. APTO research connects historical evidence with possible future states so decision-makers can reason more clearly about research, development, and innovation.
