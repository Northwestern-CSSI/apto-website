# Evidence for better technology decisions

## Introduction

APTO is a National Science Foundation program building shared evidence for understanding how technologies develop, spread, and create value. Explore the participating organizations, research projects, people, source records, and early interface concepts for tools that can make technology-outcome evidence easier to investigate.
