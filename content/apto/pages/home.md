# About the APTO Program

## Introduction

In 2023, the National Science Foundation established the Assessing and Predicting Technology Outcomes Program, or APTO for short, to develop causal models that can predict how science and technology investments shape future outcomes in technology capability, production, and use. By funding a collaborative cohort of research teams to build these models and supporting datasets and tools, APTO seeks to give decision makers the evidence base needed to strategize U.S. R&D investments effectively. The first APTO cohort includes Northwestern University, University of Chicago, Apperture.AI, Insilicom, and Macrocosm.
