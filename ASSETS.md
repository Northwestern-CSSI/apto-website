# APTO asset review register

This repository combines code, research-program content, provisional brand
marks, and visualization assets. Inclusion in the repository does not transfer
ownership or grant reuse rights.

## Brand marks

- `public/apto/nsf-logo.png` is the U.S. National Science Foundation mark. Use
  must follow the NSF Brand Standards and Usage guidance. It is not part of a
  code license.
- `public/apto/partners/` contains provisional participant marks sourced from
  participant websites or institutional brand resources for this design study.
  Confirm the approved light/dark artwork, clear space, and permission with
  Aperture, Insilicom, Macrocosm, Northwestern University, and the University
  of Chicago before production release.

## Visualization and image assets

- `science-map.png`, `model-scale.png`, `pce-prediction.png`, and `team.png`
  predate the production-candidate pass and remain in the wider 56-concept
  design study. Confirm source, consent, and redistribution rights before any
  public repository or production release; `team.png` should receive explicit
  privacy and likeness review.
- `nsf-network-art.png` is a design-study illustration. Its smaller AVIF and
  JPEG files are delivery derivatives of the same source, not separate works.
- `science-map-widget.avif` and `science-map-widget-fallback.jpg` are delivery
  derivatives of `science-map.png` for the compact widget preview.
- `public/og.png` is the design-study social image and should be replaced when
  a final APTO identity is approved.

## Illustrative interface data

The values and trajectories in the widget previews and the concepts under
`content/apto/tools/` are mock content. They are not released tools, validated
models, legal assessments, forecasts, or APTO findings. Public production work
must preserve this distinction until approved project data and tools replace
the mockups.
